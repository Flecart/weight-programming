## Design

**Strategy: stay in column 5 and dodge only vertically.** The player starts at x=5. The left, right and fire logits are set very low, so the player never leaves column 5. This makes the game one-dimensional: choose up, stay or down each step.

Two facts make this work:
- **Every row holds at most one entity.** The entity slot number fixes its row.
- **Rows with gold are safe.** A row with approaching gold has no enemy. Gold that reaches column 5 while the player waits there is collected. So the policy hops between gold rows and avoids rows where an enemy is about to reach column 5.

**Layer 1 (27 of 32 units used): per-row features, plus the player's row in binary.**

- **Units 0–7, `Dimm_r` (imminent danger in row r).** Detects an enemy at column 5, an enemy at column 4 moving right (trail at 3), or an enemy at column 6 moving left (trail at 7).
  - Rule: 2·E5 + E4 + T3 + E6 + T7 > 1.5, scaled by 8.
  - This is exactly the condition under which entering, or staying in, cell (5, r) can kill the player this step.
- **Units 8–15, `Eapp_r` (enemy approaching column 5).** Weights on the enemy and trail cells are chosen so that "trail on the outer side" (approaching) scores positive and receding scores negative.
  - The score is graded by distance d: about +1 for d ≤ 2, 0 for d = 3, and −0.96 for d ≥ 4 or an empty row.
  - Gold trails alone never activate it.
- **Units 16–23, `Gapp_r` (gold approaching column 5 or already at column 5).** Uses the same trail trick, graded so that closer gold scores higher.
  - Output by distance: d0 ≈ 0.99, d1 0.9, d2 0.76, d3 0.46, d4 0.
  - An empty row gives −0.905; receding gold or an enemy row gives ≈ −0.99.
- **Units 24–26: the player's row (1–8) as 3 binary bits** of row−1, each about ±1.

**Layer 2 (24 of 32 units used): gated action values.**
- There is one unit for each pair (player row r ∈ 1..8, action ∈ {up, stay, down}).
- **Gate:** 10·(bit pattern match) − 30. If the player is not in row r, the unit sits at −1.
- **The active unit computes tanh(V)**, where V is built from the destination row t:
  - −10 if `Dimm_t` fires (death);
  - −0.2 · `Eapp_t` (enemy coming soon);
  - +0.2 · `Gapp_t` (gold);
  - a centrality term (rows 1 and 8 cost −0.1);
  - a +0.05 bonus for staying.
- **Moves also look further ahead:**
  - Gold 2, 3 or 4 rows further in the same direction adds +0.12, +0.08 or +0.05, so the player navigates toward distant gold.
  - An approaching enemy in row r±2 subtracts 0.15. This guards against a sticky repeat of the move.
- At edge rows, up from row 1 and down from row 8 are evaluated as "stay".

**Output layer.**
- The no-op, up and down logits are 40 · Σ of their 8 units, plus 280. The +280 cancels the 7 inactive units, so each logit equals 40·tanh(V_active).
- The left, right and fire logits are fixed at −50.
- Deadly options end up at least about 11 logits below the worst safe option.
